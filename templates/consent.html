Before you begin / قبل البدء

You will learn a fictional booking task and return about seven days later. You may be offered two brief practice opportunities. Task requirements may change. We record your choices, accuracy, timing, study allocation and brief background responses.

ستتعلم مهمة حجوزات افتراضية ثم تعود بعد حوالي سبعة أيام. قد تُتاح لك فرصتان قصيرتان للتدريب، وقد تتغير متطلبات المهمة. نسجل إجاباتك ودقتها وتوقيتها والتوزيع التجريبي وبعض المعلومات العامة.

Each main session is expected to take around 20–25 minutes, subject to pilot feedback. There is no payment, employment offer or qualification. Points are task scores only. Participation is voluntary. You may stop by closing the page. Previously submitted responses remain unless withdrawn through the researcher using your study ID.
